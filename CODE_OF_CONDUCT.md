# Community conduct

Help people build useful, trustworthy travel experiences. Treat contributors and
users respectfully regardless of background, identity, experience or viewpoint.

- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect privacy, consent and the project's security-reporting boundaries.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or direct
  unwanted sexual attention at others.
- Do not post credentials, customer data or exploit details in public channels.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions,
and restrict participation when necessary. Explain moderation decisions
privately where possible and apply the same standards consistently.

Report conduct concerns privately to
[asad@noodleseed.com](mailto:asad@noodleseed.com). For vulnerability reports,
follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never publish another person's private
information as part of a report.
